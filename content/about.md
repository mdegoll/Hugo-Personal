I'm Michael DeGoll. I have spent years inside the systems mid-sized companies rent: Salesforce, integration platforms, and order-management software. I have watched them hold a business together, and I have watched them become the reason nothing can change.

Now I build the smaller tools that replace the parts that stopped earning their keep. I stay vendor-neutral because the right answer is sometimes to keep the platform, sometimes to move one workflow out, and sometimes to stop building altogether.

## Current work

[Kicksights](https://kicksights.com/) reads what is really inside a production Salesforce org: the automation, the data paths, the integration risk, and what still earns its keep. It is for teams that do not have an internal architecture group and need a plain-language map before they decide what to repair, migrate, or retire.

I also work with [Rebase](https://rebase.team/), a team of solution engineers that moves critical systems off rented platforms and onto software fitted to how a business runs. The [playbooks](/playbooks/) on this site come out of that delivery work.

## Where I'm useful

A messy process, too many disconnected tools, or a good idea that needs to become a working prototype before anyone can decide what it should be. I find the repeated work hidden inside normal operations, turn it into something a team can use, and explain the tradeoffs without burying anyone in jargon.
