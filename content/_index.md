<section class="home-section">
  <h2>How I work</h2>
  <div class="home-prose">
    <p>Most requests arrive as a software ask: a dashboard, an integration, a CRM cleanup. Underneath is usually a process nobody trusts anymore. I start there, with the workflow someone is trying to finish and the workarounds they use to finish it.</p>
    <p>If the platform you already pay for still does the job, I make it cleaner and easier to operate. If it has become drag, expensive to change and no longer matched to how the team works, I move the most painful workflow out first and build something small around it.</p>
    <p>Either way, the first release is one working slice. Real operators use it, and the next version comes from what they do with it.</p>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="home-section">
  <h2>Where AI fits</h2>
  <div class="home-prose">
